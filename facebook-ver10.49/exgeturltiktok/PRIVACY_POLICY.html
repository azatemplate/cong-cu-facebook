4. Data Collected and Used Per Feature

The extension processes data only when you explicitly use the following features. Each section describes exactly what data is involved, where it goes, and why.

4.1 Video Downloading

Trigger: You click the "Download" button on a TikTok video.
Data sent: The TikTok video URL only.
Sent to: tikwm.com, tikdownloader.io, tikvid.io, or tikmate.app (third-party download service providers, tried in that order until one responds).
Purpose: To retrieve watermark-free download links for the video, cover image, and audio streams.
Data NOT sent: No cookies, no account tokens, no user credentials, no browsing history, no personal data.
Storage: The download URL and timestamp may be saved to your local download history (Chrome local storage on your device).

4.2 Video Insight Panel

Trigger: You open the Insight Panel while viewing a TikTok video.
Data read locally: Publicly visible metadata — author name, caption, hashtags, sound name, engagement stats (likes, comments, shares, views).
Data sent: The video URL may be sent to one of the download providers listed in section 4.1 to retrieve additional metadata.
Purpose: To display enriched video insights within the extension UI.
Data NOT sent: No cookies, no account tokens, no user credentials, no personal data.
Storage: Insight data is displayed temporarily and is not persisted.

4.3 Image to Prompt / AI Analysis

Trigger: You right-click and select "Image to Prompt" or "Select Region to Prompt".
Data sent: The selected image or captured screen region only.
Sent to: The AI provider you choose:
  • Google Gemini API / Google AI Studio (via your own API key), OR
  • OpenAI API (via your own API key), OR
  • Your logged-in Gemini or ChatGPT browser tab (interacts with tab UI).
Purpose: To generate a reusable text prompt that describes the image.
Data NOT sent: No cookies, no account tokens, no browsing history, no unrelated page content.
API Key storage: Stored locally in chrome.storage.local on your device; never sent to our servers or any other third party.

4.4 Local Library (Watch History, Bookmarks, Downloads)

Data stored: Watch history entries, bookmarked videos, download history records.
Storage location: Chrome's local storage (chrome.storage.local) on your device only.
Purpose: To let you search, browse, copy links, and export your own CSV/JSON files.
Data NOT transmitted: This data never leaves your browser. Not sent to any server.

4.5 Extension Settings and Preferences

Data stored: Playback speed, equalizer presets, blocked keywords, Auto Scroll settings, UI preferences, and other configuration.
Storage location: Chrome's local storage (chrome.storage.local) on your device only.
Purpose: To remember your preferences across browser sessions.
Data NOT transmitted: Settings never leave your device.

4.6 Update Notices

Trigger: Periodically, the extension checks for update notices.
Data sent: A simple HTTP GET request to hongdolab.com to fetch a small JSON file.
Data NOT sent: No personal data, no cookies, no browsing history, no device identifiers.
Purpose: To notify you of new versions, important announcements, or support links.

4.7 Image Watermark Removal and External Video Tool

Image watermark removal: Selected PNG, JPG, and WebP files are processed inside the extension on your device and are not uploaded.
Video watermark removal: The extension does not read or process a selected video. Its visible video action is a normal link to https://hongdolab.com/.
Data sent by the link: None automatically. The extension appends no file, token, identifier, or query parameter. Data submitted on that website is governed by its own privacy terms.

9. User Rights and Controls

You have full control over your data:

  • View your data: All stored data (settings, history, bookmarks) is accessible through the extension's UI.
  • Export your data: You can export your watch history, bookmarks, and download history as CSV or JSON files.
  • Delete your data: You can clear your watch history, bookmarks, and download history from within the extension at any time.
  • Remove API keys: You can delete stored API keys from the extension settings at any time.
  • Uninstall: Uninstalling the extension removes all locally stored data automatically.
  • Opt out of features: You are never required to use download, insight, or AI analysis features. These only activate upon your explicit action.

10. Data Security

  • Since we do not collect or store any user data on external servers, there is no server-side database that could be compromised.
  • All data resides in your browser's local storage, protected by your device's security controls and Chrome's extension sandboxing.
  • All network communications with third-party services use HTTPS encryption.
  • API keys stored locally are accessible only to this extension through Chrome's storage API.
